## Brand & Style

This design system serves a mission-critical humanitarian preparedness platform. The emotional signature must balance calm authority, absolute transparency, and uncompromised dignity. In disaster nutrition, interfaces often err into either clinical coldness or alarmist catastrophe aesthetics; this system rejects both. It centers unconditional human dignity, total operational clarity, and psychological reassurance.

### Core Tenets
- **Dignified & Pity-Free:** Visual narratives avoid victimhood motifs, distress tropes, or patronizing cues. Imagery and iconography emphasize self-reliance, nutritional literacy, and community resilience.
- **Unconditional Privacy & Zero Friction:** Critical humanitarian policies are architecturally integrated into UI headers: *"Emergency food is never conditional on registration"* and *"No names. No personal data."*
- **Accessible Under duress:** Intended for users experiencing stress, fatigue, or low bandwidth. Visual elements feature oversized tap areas, generous spacing, high legibility, and full multilingual parity (including bi-directional script balance).
- **Style Archetype:** Calm Humanitarian Modernism. Combines grounded ecological greens, soft mint structural surfaces, high-contrast typography, and generous, protective corner radiuses.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an environment of stability, organic vitality, and precise operational triaging without invoking panic.

- **Primary (`#1B6B3A` - Deep Forestry Green):** The anchor of institutional trust, resilience, and authoritative structural framing. Used for primary interactive triggers, primary headings, top-level navigation, and affirmative confirmations.
- **Secondary (`#4CAF50` - Fresh Botanical Green):** Represents active sustenance, nutritional safety, and available provisions. Used for success states, nutritional surplus indicators, active triage badges, and key progress tracking.
- **Surface / Tint (`#E8F5E9` - Soft Mint Tint):** A restorative, low-glare canvas color. Replaces harsh sterile whites in high-density data areas to reduce visual fatigue during prolonged stress scenarios.
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFF` - Clean White):** Elevated container surfaces, high-clarity cards, and modal backdrops.
- **Tertiary / Attention (`#E67E22` - Alert Ochre):** Warm amber for non-blocking advisories, storage shelf-life limits, boil-water notices, and preparation warnings without causing panic.
- **Error / Severe Warning (`#D32F2F` - Warning Red):** Reserved strictly for critical life-safety hazards, lethal allergen warnings, spoilage notices, or emergency route failures.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#212B24`):** A forest-tinted slate neutral providing optimal WCAG AAA contrast against mint and white surfaces, avoiding the harshness of pitch black.

## Typography

The platform specifies **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic levels. Its wide apertures, clean geometric base, and tall x-height guarantee immediate legibility across varied languages, non-Latin script alignments, and under low-contrast sunlight conditions outdoors.

- **Display & Headline Levels:** Feature tight tracking with authoritative weights (700–800) to project structural stability and reassure displaced users seeking immediate answers.
- **Body Text:** Maintained at generous line-height ratios (1.5x minimum) to prevent optical crowding and support rapid scanning by users in states of cognitive overload.
- **Multilingual Support:** Diacritical clearance is native to all headline line-heights, ensuring languages such as Spanish, French, Arabic, and Vietnamese render cleanly without line collisions.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid anchors the platform on desktop (max content container: 1200px), reflowing to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Humanitarian Density Strategy
- **Generous Spatial Buffers:** High visual breathing room minimizes cognitive clutter. Cramped layouts escalate stress; spacing is therefore treated as an active calming mechanism.
- **Accessible Touch Geometry:** All interactive targets (buttons, selector chips, inputs) maintain a minimum hit boundary of 48px × 48px, with desktop and mobile spacing tokens ensuring adjacent links do not trigger accidental taps under physical trembling or wet-screen scenarios.
- **Policy Priority Layout:** Persistent humanitarian guarantee banners sit permanently docked directly below primary navigational controls, spanning the full container width before content cards engage.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal layering and soft ambient depth, rejecting harsh synthetic drop-shadows or jarring skeuomorphism.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base background tinted with `#E8F5E9` (Soft Mint Tint) to create a protective, low-glare foundation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces layered directly above the canvas, bounded by a 1px border of `#1B6B3A` at 12% opacity. Shadows are ultra-diffuse: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 107, 58, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Sheets & Modals):** Elevated utility surfaces rendered in `#FFFFFF`, supported by a deeper tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 107, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Emergency Priority Overlays:** Sticky banners and alert dialogs utilize high-contrast solid backgrounds with no ambient translucency, ensuring critical text is legible over moving background content.

## Shapes

The design system implements a rounded, protective geometry to reinforce safety and accessibility. 

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Configured strictly to `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px radius), creating organic, approachable physical perimeters that avoid clinical sharpness.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Standardized to `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to match the curvature cadence of larger cards without collapsing into full pill shapes.
- **Status Indicators & Chips:** Maintained at `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) for compact efficiency and clear category differentiation.

## Components

### Humanitarian Guarantee Banner
- **Visuals:** Full-width strip using `#1B6B3A` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography.
- **Content Enforcement:** Displays two uncompromised commitments:
  1. *"Emergency food is never conditional on registration."*
  2. *"No names. No personal data."*
- **Icons:** Lock-free, open-hand shield iconography in `#4CAF50`. No account creation or profiling steps can obscure this block.

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary:** Solid `#1B6B3A` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-xl`, min-height 52px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Hover/Focus shifts to a deeper forest green shade with a distinct 3px outline in `#4CAF50` at 40% offset.
- **Secondary / Offline Sync:** Tinted `#E8F5E9` background, `#1B6B3A` text, 1.5px border `#1B6B3A` at 25% opacity.
- **Emergency Action:** Solid `#D32F2F` fill, white text, strictly isolated for critical resource distress alerts.

### Nutrition & Ration Cards
- **Construction:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `rounded-2xl` corners, 1px border (`#1B6B3A` at 10% opacity), internal padding `space-lg`.
- **Content Model:** Caloric metrics, dietary classification tags (e.g., Halal, Kosher, Vegan, Infant Safe, Allergen-Free), preparation requirements (no-cook vs. boiling water required), and current distribution status.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Form Controls:** 52px height minimum, `rounded-xl` corners, `#FFFFFF` background, solid 1.5px border `#212B24` (30% opacity). Focus state expands to 2px solid `#1B6B3A` with no disruptive displacement.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 24px × 24px hit targets with a 48px outer tap boundary. Selected state fills with `#1B6B3A` and displays a bold white checkmark.

### Category Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Status Chips:** `rounded-lg`, height 36px, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Neutral state: `#E8F5E9` background with `#1B6B3A` text. Selected state: `#1B6B3A` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Alert Chips (Allergens / Boil Water):** Warm `#E67E22` or `#D32F2F` at 12% tint background with heavy-weight matching foreground text.

### Language & Locale Selector
- **Persistent Header Module:** Quick-switch menu featuring native script names (e.g., "Español", "العربية", "Tiếng Việt") rather than country flags, preventing political alienation and accelerating discovery.
